# Talking points and slurs

Each entry: what it claims or does, who documents it, and what we measured. Rebuttals are linked, not rewritten. Counts are explained in [measurements.md](measurements.md).

## Slurs

### "Troon" / "troons"

- **What it is:** a slur for transgender people. Listed with "tranny" in Beth's [Transphobia Glossary](https://bethylamine.github.io/library/glossary/) (checked).
- **Measured:** about 2,550 original posts a day on X in English before 8 October 2026 (daily range 1,713 to 4,321), plus thousands of reposts. About 10 a day on Bluesky.

### "Groomer"

- **What it does:** paints LGBTQ+ people as child abusers.
- **Documented by:** CCDH and the Human Rights Campaign (2022) counted 989,547 tweets using slurs such as "groomer" about LGBTQ+ people in January to July 2022, and a 406% rise in "grooming" talk in the month after Florida's "Don't Say Gay or Trans" law (checked).
- **Measured:** about 41 posts a day on Bluesky; a hand check of 47 posts found none using it this way (mostly pet grooming and abuse call-outs). Not yet counted on X.

### "Zippertits"

- **What it is:** a slur for trans men, about mastectomy scars. In Beth's glossary (checked). Not measured.

## Claims

### "There's no such thing as trans (kids)"

- **What it claims:** that trans people, or trans children, do not exist.
- **Documented by:** The 74 (14 December 2023) reported a New York City education council member writing "There is no such thing as trans kids" in a parents' group chat; the city's education department called the remarks "despicable" (checked). This is a single documented incident, not research on how common the claim is. Beth's library has no page dedicated to it.
- **Measured:** about 150 original posts a day on X ("no such thing as trans" or "no such thing as a trans", English, before 8 October 2026; range 72 to 243). Under 2 a day on Bluesky.

### Bottom surgery as a "wound"

- **What it claims:** that vaginoplasty leaves a wound the body keeps trying to close.
- **Documented by:** LGBTQ Nation (Gwendolyn Smith, 11 April 2019) reports Christine Burns MBE describing it as a myth; dilators are needed while healing and are largely replaced by ordinary activity later (checked). Burns is reported to trace the claim to Germaine Greer's *The Whole Woman* (*unverified*: seen only in a search summary).
- **Measured:** too rare to track. "Gaping wound" with a trans term appeared about 2 times a day on X; the phrases are mostly used about injuries, games and fiction.

### "Gender ideology"

- **What it does:** frames trans people as an ideology or movement rather than people.
- **Documented by:** GLAAD's online-hate glossary has an entry (*unverified*: not opened). Beth's glossary has "Transgender ideology / trans-rights activist (TRA)" (checked: the entry exists).
- **Measured:** about 15 posts a day on Bluesky in English; 0 to 5 in German, French, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese. Not yet counted on X.

### "Protect women" / women's sports

- **What it claims:** that trans women threaten women's safety or fairness in sport.
- **Documented by:** Beth's [GC Arguments](https://bethylamine.github.io/library/gc-arguments/) has pages on sports participation and on the claim that women's and trans rights clash (checked: pages listed).
- **Measured:** on Bluesky, "women's sports" about 82 a day and "protect women" about 35, but a hand check found 1 to 3 of 50 and 0 to 2 of 48 posts using the framing; the rest were fans or critics. Not yet counted on X.

### Other gender-critical terms counted on Bluesky

"Gender critical" (about 20 a day), "AGP" (about 24), "what is a woman" (about 6), "trans ideology" (about 6), "biological male" (about 3), "adult human female" and "autogynephilia" (about 2). Beth's library covers autogynephilia, "What is a woman?" and the film *Adult Human Female* (checked: pages listed). None counted on X yet.

## Influence-operation topics

### "Trans youth", "Woke Wars"

- **Documented by:** DFRLab (September 2024): a fake LGBTQ+ outlet run by the Russian operation Doppelganger filed stories under the topics "Trans youth" and "Woke Wars" (checked).
- **Measured:** "trans youth" about 27 posts a day on Bluesky; "woke" about 1,826. Both count every use of the words, so they measure the topic, not the operation.
